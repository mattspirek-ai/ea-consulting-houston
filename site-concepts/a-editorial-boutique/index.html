Questions, answered plainly

The six things everyone asks.

What is the contract length?

Month to month. There is no annual commitment, no minimum term beyond the current month, and no buyout fee if you later hire your assistant or wind down. You can cancel anytime. Some national firms require twelve-month contracts with five-figure buyout fees; we built our terms specifically so you never face that.

How do you keep quality consistent?

Three ways. First, we only place senior assistants — eight to fifteen-plus years supporting executives — who pass a background check, reference checks, and a skills assessment. Second, backup coverage is standard, so vacations and sick days do not stall your desk. Third, if a match is not working, we re-match within five business days at no charge, and a two-week satisfaction checkpoint plus monthly reviews catch problems early.

How is confidentiality handled?

A confidentiality agreement is standard on every engagement, before any work begins. Assistants are background-checked and work under our device and password policies. Many of our clients operate in sectors where discretion is the job — deal flow, patient schedules, client matters — and our practices are built for that. Details are on our Security & Confidentiality page.

Are there hidden fees?

No. The prices on this page are the prices. Beyond your monthly tier, the only possible charges are ones you choose: extra hours at $65/hr, specialist work at $79/hr, Houston in-person days at $450/day, and a one-time $299 onboarding fee, which is waived on Chief of Staff. Unused hours roll over one month, so a light month is not money lost.

What about time zones?

We are a Houston firm on Central Time. Your assistant works your business hours — morning inbox triage happens in your morning, and a 4:45 p.m. schedule change gets handled that afternoon. If you want an assistant physically present for board weeks or events, in-person days are available across greater Houston.

I have never had an EA. How do I know what to delegate?

You do not have to know — that is the point of the 30-Day Delegation Playbook, which every engagement starts with. Week by week, we move work over in a deliberate order: calendar and inbox first, then travel and meeting prep, then the recurring operational work specific to your role. By day thirty you have a running system, not a to-do list you have to manage.
