The questions executives actually ask

Before you book

What am I committing to?
One month at a time. Every plan is month-to-month with a 2-week satisfaction guarantee, and there is no buyout fee if you later hire your EA or move on. Unused hours roll over one month.
How do you keep quality consistent?
Every EA is senior — 8 to 15+ years supporting executives — background-checked, reference-checked, and skills-assessed. You get backup coverage when your EA is out, a free re-match within 5 business days if the fit is wrong, and a monthly review to keep the work sharp.
How is confidentiality handled?
A confidentiality agreement is standard on every engagement, before any work begins. EAs follow a device and password policy, and are placed with sector-appropriate discretion — deal terms, patient context, and client matters stay where they belong.
Are there fees beyond the published price?
Only what's on this page: your plan, a $299 one-time onboarding (waived on Chief of Staff), and optional add-ons — extra hours at $65/hr, specialist work at $79/hr, in-person Houston days at $450/day. Nothing else.
What about time zones?
We're Houston-rooted and work Central Time by default, aligned to your day rather than trailing it. If you want an EA physically present — a board day, an event, an office reset — in-person Houston days are available at $450/day.
I don't know what to delegate. Is that a problem?
It's the most common starting point. Every engagement includes the 30-Day Delegation Playbook: a week-by-week plan your EA runs with you, starting with calendar, inbox, and travel, then expanding into your sector's real work. You don't have to arrive with a job description.
