What buyers say about the alternatives

Documented complaints, and the term that answers each one.

Paraphrased from published reviews and independent analyses — each links to its source. The right column is not marketing copy; it is the specific contractual term above that removes the complaint.

Reported about BELAY · pricing opacity

Pricing is not published anywhere on the website — buyers must sit through a sales consultation before learning what it costs.

Source: usecarly.com BELAY review · verified 2026-09-07

Our answer

All three tiers, all four add-ons, and every term are printed on this page, with a changelog below. No call required to see a number.

Reported about BELAY · contract lock-in

The standard contract is reported as 12 months with 30 days' notice — long-term lock-in is a major friction point for buyers.

Source: usecarly.com BELAY review · verified 2026-09-07

Our answer

Month-to-month, cancel anytime. If we're only good for 90 days, we only deserve 90 days.

Reported about BELAY · buyout fee

A buyout fee of $10,000–$20,000 is reported if a client wants to hire their assistant directly, contractually enforced after the engagement ends.

Source: usecarly.com BELAY review · verified 2026-09-07

Our answer

No buyout fee — $0, in writing. If the right long-term outcome is your EA joining your payroll, that is a success, not a penalty event.

Reported industry-wide · hours expiry

Unused hours expire at the end of each month with no rollover — clients pay for time they never received.

Source: solveline.pro BELAY pricing analysis · verified 2026-09-07

Our answer

Unused hours roll over one month. A light August carries into September.

Reported about BELAY & Wing · quality inconsistency

Quality is described as "hit or miss" depending on the match — BELAY's Trustpilot shows a polarized 3.4/5 — and Wing reviewers report underqualified assistants and high turnover.

Source: usecarly.com review · verified 2026-09-07 Source: Trustpilot, Wing Assistant · verified 2026-09-07

Our answer

Every EA passes the five-step vetting below (8–15+ years required), and the risk of a bad match sits with us: 2-week satisfaction guarantee, replacement within 5 business days, backup coverage as standard.

Reported industry-wide · delegation left to the client

Even at $2,000–$5,000/month, clients report still doing most of the operational lifting themselves — no structured framework for what to hand off.

Source: solveline.pro pricing analysis · verified 2026-09-07

Our answer

Every engagement starts with the 30-Day Delegation Playbook — a week-by-week handoff plan your EA runs, so the delegating is done with you, not left to you.

How we vet

Five gates before anyone touches your calendar.

01

Background check

Criminal and identity screening on every executive assistant, refreshed on a set cycle.

02

Reference checks

Structured references from prior executives supported — not character references, work references.

03

Skills assessment

Live scenario work: calendar triage under conflict, inbox-to-decision drafting, travel rebooking under pressure.

04

8–15+ years experience

Senior EAs only. No junior generalists, no shared assistant pools, no anonymous benches.

05

Sector tracks

Placement into an industry track — energy, healthcare/TMC, legal, private capital, founders — so day one uses your vocabulary: AFE routing, credentialing, docket-aware calendaring, LP communications.
