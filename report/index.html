Table-stakes promises (everyone makes them)

  • Matched in 24-48 hours (Zirtual, Double, Wing, Viva Talent, Assist World)
  • US-based college-educated assistants (BELAY, Boldly, Zirtual, Time etc, Worxbee, Squared Away)
  • Top X% of applicants accepted (ranges from 0.5% to 3% — all claim selectivity)
  • No long-term contracts / cancel anytime (nearly universal except Athena)
  • Free re-match / replacement guarantee (nearly universal)
  • AI-fluent / AI-enhanced assistants (BELAY, Boldly, Athena, Wing, Double)
  • Save 15-40 hours per month (common ROI claim)
  • Dedicated (not pooled) assistant (premium tier differentiator vs Fancy Hands, Magic pooled)

Top buyer objections (synthesized from reviews)

  • Too expensive compared to hiring in-house at the fraction being offered
  • Quality varies by which VA you get — no guarantee of consistent experience
  • Long-term contracts (especially BELAY's 12-month) create fear of being locked in with a poor match
  • Offshore VAs have timezone and communication barriers for real-time executive support
  • Onboarding takes too long — executives want help immediately, not after 2-4 weeks
  • Hard to know what to delegate — buyers want a service that helps them figure that out, not just execution
  • No industry-specific knowledge — 'My VA doesn't understand oil and gas terminology'
  • Concern about data security and confidentiality when sharing executive-level information with remote staff
  • Hidden fees (setup fees, buyout fees, credit card processing) increase true cost
  • Previous bad experience with a VA service — gun-shy about trying again

What buyers complain about

Count of 18 sourced pain points by theme (Trustpilot, Indeed, review articles). Full quotes and URLs in the workbook 'Buyer Signals' sheet.

Gaps nobody fills

  • Local in-person hybrid support: no national player offers blended remote + local presence in major metros (Houston, Dallas, etc.) — pure remote is universal
  • Industry-specific EA agencies for specific verticals (legal, healthcare, real estate EA specifically at executive level) — most offer these as add-ons not specialization
  • Transparent pricing + US-based talent under $2,000/month: the under-$2K US-based tier has only Time etc (lower seniority) — the premium US-based EA space has a $2,600 floor
  • On-demand / surge capacity with US talent: no US-based service offers true on-demand hourly without a monthly commitment
  • Executive community / peer matching: no service matches executives with EAs from their own industry or peer networks
  • Risk-free trial with full money-back: most offer rematches but not true money-back guarantees for unhappy clients after work begins
  • Hybrid concierge model (local errands + remote EA): international companies like DonnaPro target this but no US domestic player serves the in-person hybrid need
  • Certified EA specializations: no player prominently features certified/credentialed EAs (PACE, CAP certification) as a differentiator
  • Fractional EA specifically for Houston or Texas market: no geo-targeted brand serves this niche nationally while emphasizing local cultural/timezone alignment

Why this is defensible

Local is unclaimed

No national player has Houston presence or an in-person option; local agencies don't say it confidently and rank on template sites.

Transparency is traffic

The biggest US brand hides pricing behind a sales call and locks in for 12 months. Pricing and comparison SERPs are held by aggregators, not agencies.

Industry fluency is a stated need

“My VA doesn't understand oil and gas terminology” is a recorded objection. Houston has 19 energy Fortune 500 HQs, the TMC, 4,215 law offices, and a deep private-capital base.

The bar is low locally

Local sites score 2–3/5. A professional site with public pricing and vertical pages clears the Houston field on day one.
