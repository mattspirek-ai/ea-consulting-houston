# Fractional EA Business — Houston + Virtual Market Entry Strategy

## Executive Summary

**The opportunity in one paragraph.** The national virtual-EA market is crowded at two poles — offshore managed services (Wing, Prialto, Athena, Magic; ~$700–3,000/mo) and US-based premium agencies (Boldly, BELAY, Worxbee, Delegate Solutions; hard floor ~$2,600/mo, $65–92/hr effective). Every national player treats Houston as a zip code; none has local presence, industry fluency, or an in-person option. Houston's own market is thin: two verifiable local EA agencies (Robert Lee & Associates on Wix with no pricing; Sky Virtual Solutions on a Ueni builder), a handful of LinkedIn-only solo operators, and strong staffing firms (Burnett, Murray, Frontline, Robert Half) that only place W-2 in-person hires. **No one occupies the intersection of Houston credibility × US-based senior talent × published pricing × industry fluency (energy, healthcare/TMC, legal, PE/family office).** That intersection is the recommended position.

**Recommended move:** launch a premium boutique fractional EA firm with three published month-to-month tiers ($1,200 / $2,300 / $3,300 for 20 / 40 / 60 hrs), no buyout fees, a 5-business-day match promise, a 2-week satisfaction guarantee, and an optional Houston in-person day add-on. Win locally first (LinkedIn + referral + Houston landing pages, where national brands have zero local pages), then extend virtually via comparison and pricing content where BELAY's opacity leaves traffic on the table.

**Data caveats:** traffic figures are approximate (Exa entity data, ~Jun 2026); keyword volumes could not be sourced from free tools and must be pulled from Google Keyword Planner; the '547 employed' Houston EA figure is unverified. See workbook README.

## Ideal Customer Profile

**Primary ICP — the Houston executive who has outgrown self-management but can't justify a $99K fully-loaded hire.**

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Title | Founder/CEO of a $2–50M company; managing partner or practice leader; PE/family-office principal; physician-executive or clinic owner; energy VP/GM running a business unit |
| Company size | 5–150 employees, or a partner-level individual inside a larger firm without dedicated support |
| Geography | Houston metro first (Downtown, Galleria/Uptown, Energy Corridor, TMC, The Woodlands, Sugar Land, Katy); then Texas; then national virtual |
| Trigger events | New funding or acquisition; EA resigned; calendar chaos; travel-heavy quarter; spouse/office manager doing EA work; failed offshore VA experience |
| Budget reality | Willing to spend $1,200–3,500/mo; anchored against a $68K salary + 30% benefits + overhead (~$8,250/mo derived) |
| What they value | Discretion, judgment, US business-hours availability, industry vocabulary, someone who can walk into a Houston office when needed |
| What they fear | Contract lock-in (BELAY 12-month), hit-or-miss quality, offshore timezone gaps, hidden fees, data confidentiality |

**Houston demand context (sourced):** 268,670 management-occupation workers; 26 Fortune 500 HQs (19 energy); ~250,000 energy professionals earning avg $141.7K; 20,443 licensed physicians; 4,215 legal establishments; 109 banks with a substantial PE/family-office community (GHP Houston Facts 2024/2026; Dallas Fed). Softer 2025 job growth (0.5%) and oil & gas layoffs favor flexible/fractional spend over W-2 hires.

**Secondary ICP (virtual, national):** founders and executives who searched "Belay alternatives", "Boldly vs Belay", or "executive assistant pricing" and were put off by unpublished pricing or long contracts.

**Anti-ICP:** solopreneurs wanting <10 hrs/mo of task work (Fancy Hands / Time etc territory); companies wanting a $9/hr offshore VA; anyone needing bookkeeping or marketing execution as the primary need.

## Positioning & Messaging

**Positioning statement (working):**
> [BrandName] is Houston's fractional executive assistant firm. We pair senior, US-based executive assistants who speak energy, healthcare, legal, and private-capital fluently with leaders who need real support — published pricing, month-to-month, matched in five business days, and in the room when you need us.

**Category:** Fractional Executive Assistant firm (not "virtual assistant" — the VA label drags you into the $9/hr comparison set). Use "fractional EA" as the primary term, "virtual executive assistant" as the SEO secondary.

**Four pillars (each one is a gap the research found empty):**
1. **Houston-rooted.** Local references, local office hours, optional in-person days. No national competitor offers this; local competitors don't say it confidently.
2. **Senior, US-based, industry-fluent.** 8–15+ years, W-2 or vetted 1099, with vertical tracks (energy, TMC/healthcare, legal, PE/family office). Buyers explicitly complain "my VA doesn't understand oil and gas terminology."
3. **Radically transparent terms.** Prices on the website. Month-to-month. No buyout fee if you hire your EA (BELAY charges $10–20K). No expiring-hours gotchas — offer 1-month rollover.
4. **Delegation, done with you.** A structured 30-day onboarding playbook (buyers say premium services still make them figure out what to delegate).

**Table-stakes claims you must also make (they're universal, not differentiators):** matched in ≤5 business days, backup coverage, cancel anytime, free re-match, background-checked, AI-fluent.

**Tone:** calm, senior, discreet — a chief-of-staff voice, not a gig-economy voice. Avoid "hustle", "crush your to-do list", stock photos of headsets.

**Proof to build in the first 90 days:** 3 named Houston testimonials with title + industry; a public "hours saved" tally; EA bios with years and sectors; a signed NDA/confidentiality standard page.

## Offer Ladder & Pricing

**Recommended: Option A — Premium boutique** (derived from sourced benchmarks; see workbook 'Pricing Recommendation' sheet).

| Tier | Hours/mo | Monthly | Effective $/hr | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 20 | $1,200 | $60 | Partner-level individual, first-time delegator |
| Executive | 40 | $2,300 | $57.50 | CEO/founder with calendar, travel, inbox, board prep |
| Chief of Staff | 60 | $3,300 | $55 | Leader running a team, events, investor/board cadence |
| Add-ons | — | Houston in-person day $450/day; extra hours $65/hr; specialist work (deck design, PM) $79/hr; onboarding $299 one-time (waived on Chief of Staff) | | |

**Why these numbers:** undercuts Boldly ($65/hr flat, $2,600 floor) and Delegate Solutions ($88–92/hr) while sitting above BELAY's estimated $42–50/hr — so you are cheaper than the premium brands on paper and visibly more transparent than the biggest brand. Each tier is a fraction of the ~$8,250/mo derived cost of an in-house Houston EA.

**Margin check (do this before launch):** at $57.50/hr effective, paying a senior Houston EA $32–38/hr as a 1099 contractor (or ~$45/hr fully loaded W-2) leaves a 22–40% gross margin. Boldly runs W-2 at $65/hr. If you want W-2 quality with W-2 margins, either price at $65/hr (parity with Boldly, win on local + transparency) or start 1099 and convert to W-2 as volume grows. Recommend launching at the table above with 1099 senior contractors and revisiting at 10 clients.

**Terms that become marketing:** month-to-month · 2-week satisfaction guarantee (refund the first two weeks if not delighted — Boldly's lifetime guarantee is the benchmark buyers praise) · unused hours roll over one month · no buyout fee, ever · confidentiality agreement standard · replacement within 5 business days.

**Option B (mid-market, $900/$1,800/$2,600)** exists in the workbook as a fallback if early sales calls show price resistance — but it erodes the premium story and margin. Do not lead with it.

## Go-to-Market Channels

Ranked by expected payback for a Houston-first launch.

1. **Founder-led LinkedIn (weeks 1–12, daily).** The Houston fractional-EA conversation currently lives on LinkedIn among solo operators. Post 3–5x/week: delegation playbooks, "what a Houston energy VP's week looks like with an EA", pricing transparency takes. Connect with 30 Houston executives/day in target verticals. This is the fastest path to the first 3 clients.
2. **Warm referral engine.** Houston EA staffing firms (overflow they can't place fractionally), executive coaches, wealth managers, CPA firms, law-firm administrators, Houston chapter of IAAP/ASAP, Greater Houston Partnership and vertical associations (HBA, TMC Innovation, IPAA/energy groups). Offer a 10% first-year referral fee.
3. **Local SEO landing pages (build week 1, compound for 12 months).** "Executive assistant Houston", "virtual executive assistant Houston", "fractional executive assistant Houston" have no national brand landing pages ranking — job boards and staffing firms fill the SERP. Add suburb pages (The Woodlands, Sugar Land, Katy, Energy Corridor) and vertical pages. Google Business Profile with a real Houston address.
4. **Comparison and pricing content (national virtual).** "Belay alternatives", "Boldly vs Belay", "how much does an executive assistant cost", "fractional EA cost" are owned by aggregators, not agencies. Publish honest comparison pages and a live pricing calculator — BELAY's opacity is your traffic.
5. **Google Ads (month 2+, small).** Exact-match Houston terms only; national terms are expensive against BELAY/Boldly budgets.
6. **Partnerships with coworking/EO/YPO/Vistage chapters in Houston** for lunch-and-learns on delegation.

De-prioritize: Thumbtack/Bark (drags into $9/hr comparisons), Upwork, cold email.

## 90-Day Launch Plan

**Days 1–30 — Foundation**
- Entity, insurance (E&O + cyber), standard MSA + NDA, 1099 contractor agreement with non-solicit but **no client buyout fee**.
- Recruit bench of 4–6 senior Houston EAs (energy, healthcare, legal, PE tracks); background checks; bios with years + sectors.
- Build website per blueprint (pricing page live on day 1), Google Business Profile, LinkedIn company page.
- Write the 30-day Delegation Playbook (PDF lead magnet + onboarding tool).
- Pull Google Keyword Planner volumes for the 61 keywords in the workbook; verify Houston BLS wage/employment figures.
- Founder LinkedIn cadence starts; 3 discovery conversations/week.

**Days 31–60 — First clients**
- Target: 3 paying clients (mix of tiers) at full price; 1 in-person hybrid.
- Publish 4 Houston/suburb landing pages + 2 vertical pages + first 2 comparison articles.
- Referral agreements signed with 5 partners (staffing firm, coach, CPA, wealth manager, law-firm admin).
- Collect first testimonial with name, title, industry within 21 days of each start.

**Days 61–90 — Proof and repeat**
- Target: 6–8 clients, ≥1 Chief of Staff tier; first EA at 2 clients (utilization model proven).
- Publish pricing calculator + "hours saved" counter; case study #1.
- Start small Houston-only Google Ads test ($1–1.5K/mo).
- Review margin per tier; decide W-2 conversion path.

**Kill criteria:** if by day 90 there are <3 paying clients after ≥40 discovery calls, revisit pricing (Option B) or ICP before spending on ads.

## Risks & Open Questions

- **Margin squeeze:** premium price with senior US talent is thin at 1099 rates ~$35/hr; plan the W-2 conversion or $65/hr parity move early.
- **Bench utilization:** month-to-month terms mean revenue can drop fast; keep EAs on 2 clients each and a 2-week notice clause.
- **Non-solicit without buyout fee:** legal review needed — the "no buyout fee" promise is a differentiator but exposes you to clients hiring your EAs; mitigate with a modest, disclosed conversion fee (e.g., one month's retainer) if counsel insists, and still market it as far below BELAY's $10–20K.
- **SEO data gap:** all keyword volumes are unknown; Houston-specific fractional EA searches may be small — local SEO is a compounding bet, LinkedIn/referral is the near-term engine.
- **Consolidation:** PennSpring's Zirtual roll-up could add local SEO pages quickly; own the Houston SERP before they notice.
- **Unverified stat:** the '547 employed' Houston EA figure must not be used publicly until checked against BLS OEWS.
- **Open:** brand name; W-2 vs 1099 at launch; whether to include light bookkeeping/travel-booking as add-ons; Spanish-bilingual EA track (Houston demographic advantage, untested).
